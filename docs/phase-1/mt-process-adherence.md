# MT Process Adherence

**Route:** `/mt-process-adherence`  
**Purpose:** Assess batch facilitation against eight module benchmarks.

## Filters

Date master filter with Overall, Single Date, and Date Range modes. All dashboard metrics and tables respond to the selected date scope.

## Snapshot

- Average Process Adherence, out of 8 modules
- Most Defaulting Module, including the percentage of batches below benchmark

## Sections and tables

### Process Adherence (N = batches in scope)

Distribution chart by the number of modules adhered to. The X-axis is **No. of Modules Adhered To**, with buckets from 0 Modules through Maximum Modules.

### Batch Benchmark Adherence by Module (N = batches in scope)

100% stacked chart by module.

- Green: Batches Meeting Benchmark
- Red: Batches Not Meeting Benchmark

### Process Adherence Summary by Geography

Hierarchical geography table: Division → District → Taluka.

Columns: No. of Batches, each 0/8 through 8/8 bucket, Average Process Adherence.

### Exercise-wise Process Adherence

Module and exercise drill-down with Benchmark Timing, Average Time Spent, and percentage of batches below benchmark.

## Business rules

- Process adherence is the number of the eight modules that a batch met.
- The most defaulting module is the module missed by the most in-scope batches.
